<div class="section-heading">
<p class="about-kicker">My Story</p>
<h2 id="story-title">From curiosity to applied AI</h2>
<p>My path was not a straight line. It moved through curiosity, lost years, late-night rebuilding, production work, teaching, setbacks, and a renewed hunger to learn.</p>
</div>
<div class="story-tree" aria-label="Career story timeline">
<article class="story-item story-left">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2015</span>
<p class="story-stage">First spark</p>
</div>
<h3>A movie that opened a door</h3>
<p>After watching "Who Am I", I became fascinated by computers, security, and the hidden systems behind the internet. I did not become the hacker I imagined, but those years taught me how powerful curiosity can be when it refuses to stay quiet.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-right">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2017</span>
<p class="story-stage">University</p>
</div>
<h3>Ambition met reality</h3>
<p>I entered Danang University of Science and Technology with big expectations, but I was not ready for the discipline that growth requires. Those years were not impressive on paper, but they became an honest baseline: talent without consistency does not compound.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-left">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2020</span>
<p class="story-stage">Rebuild</p>
</div>
<h3>The lockdown that changed my direction</h3>
<p>Covid isolation unexpectedly gave me focus. I discovered AI, learned Python seriously, built neural networks, and spent late nights following tutorials line by line. For the first time, I gave a technical subject my full attention.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-right">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2021</span>
<p class="story-stage">FTECH</p>
</div>
<h3>From intern to AI engineer</h3>
<p>I joined FTECH as an intern and grew into an AI Engineer over the following years. It was the place that gave me room to learn, ship, fail, and mature. Much of my production mindset was shaped there.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-left">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2024</span>
<p class="story-stage">Teaching</p>
</div>
<h3>Learning by teaching</h3>
<p>I began teaching programming to primary and secondary students. It became more than a side job: explaining Scratch, Python, and algorithms to young learners sharpened my own thinking and reminded me to work with responsibility.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-right">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2025</span>
<p class="story-stage">Transition</p>
</div>
<h3>A difficult year, a wider world</h3>
<p>After leaving FTECH during a layoff, I tried new companies, started freelancing on Upwork, and learned how different the market feels outside a familiar environment. It was unstable, but it widened my view of products, clients, and ownership.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-left">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2026</span>
<p class="story-stage">Mynavi</p>
</div>
<h3>A new environment for research and growth</h3>
<p>I joined Mynavi TechTus Vietnam as an AI Engineer, working with supportive people and gaining space to study, research, and explore current AI trends instead of being locked into a single narrow project.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-right">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">2026</span>
<p class="story-stage">Graduate study</p>
</div>
<h3>Returning to the fundamentals</h3>
<p>I started a master's program in Mechatronics at DUT because I felt a genuine hunger for knowledge, including things I should have learned more deeply years earlier. It is late, but it is not too late.</p>
</div>
</article>
<article class="story-item story-left">
<div class="story-card">
<div class="story-card-top"><span class="story-year">Now</span>
<p class="story-stage">Applied AI</p>
</div>
<h3>Helping people and businesses use AI well</h3>
<p>Today I focus on applied AI, research, AI literacy for working professionals, productivity improvement, and digital transformation consulting for businesses. I am always open to meaningful collaboration.</p>
</div>
</article>
